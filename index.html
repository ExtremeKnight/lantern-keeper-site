How a night goes

Defend. Choose. Grow. Return.

Every night is a new run. Every run leaves you stronger. Easy to start in a minute, deep enough to master for months.

  1. Defend the light

    Your lantern fires on its own. Tap the creatures that slip through, and trigger your keeper's ability when the fog closes in.

  2. Choose 1 of 3

    After every wave, pick an upgrade. Burning bolts, frost halos, split beams: find the combos that turn a lamp into a storm.

  3. Face the guardians

    Every fifth wave a guardian rises from the deep. Survive it and the night goes on, harder and richer.

  4. Grow and return

    Embers from every night buy lasting upgrades in the Workshop, and your lantern keeps earning a little while you're away.

Features

A whole coast to keep

From a quiet first night on the Fogbound Coast to the edge of the world, there is always another region to chart, another build to try and another keeper to meet.

25 regions

Chart the fog, region by region

Each region has its own sky, its own creatures and its own rule. Whiteouts in the Frozen Reach shorten your lantern's reach. Gusts in the Jade Tempest push creatures sideways. In the Ember Empire, elites march more often and burn hotter.

  • Route maps with battles, elites, events, treasure, merchants, rescues and a guardian at the end
  • Endless nights, a daily challenge, weekly goals, a Boss Rush and Ascension levels
  • A Codex of every creature, guardian, weapon and place you have seen
Your build, every night

Build a lantern no one has seen

Pick a keeper and a weapon, slot your charms, then shape the night one upgrade at a time. Upgrades level up and combine into synergies, so two nights are never quite alike.

  • 17 keepers, each with their own ability, and 31 weapons from the Flare Pistol to the First Flame
  • 27 charms and dozens of upgrades with named synergies
  • A Workshop of lasting upgrades, and a lighthouse to relight and climb
Together or head to head

Up to four keepers, one lighthouse

Light the way together in co-op: every keeper fights with their own lantern, weapon, ability and upgrades, and the team votes on what comes next. Or prove your build in ranked 1v1 PvP.

  • Co-op online with a room code, on the same Wi-Fi, or phone to phone with a QR code
  • Server-run nights, where no player's device decides for the others
  • Ranked PvP with ratings and a leaderboard. Every device plays a match exactly the same
Between the nights

Meet other keepers in the Lantern Bazaar

Walk a lantern-lit harbour where keepers gather. Swap items on the Market, craft boosts at the Workbench, trade with friends, and show off your look.

  • A walkable 3D harbour, live with other signed-in keepers
  • Friends, parties, chat, trading and the Market, with blocking and reporting built in
  • Festivals, collections and music you unlock by playing
Make it yours

A keeper and a lighthouse to call your own

Coats, hats, scarves, lantern beams, tower stripes, whole lighthouses, profile banners and frames. Most are earned in the game. A few are sold, and they only ever change how things look.

  • Hundreds of combinations, every one tested to look right together
  • A public profile with your title, badges and the achievements you choose to show

Our promise

Fair by design

Lantern Keeper is free, and it stays fair. Everything that makes you stronger is earned by playing. In PvP, looks are only looks: the same build plays exactly the same whatever it wears, on every device.

The game also respects your time and your privacy: no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or trackers, and you can play the whole game without an account.

  • Never pay-to-winAnything sold is cosmetic. Boosts work only in PvE and are never sold for money.
  • No ads, everNo banners, no videos to watch for rewards, no interruptions.
  • Your data stays yoursNo trackers. Cloud saves are optional, and you can delete your account from inside the game.
  • Plays offlineInstall it from the browser or download the app, and play anywhere.
For everyone

One save, every device

Questions

Good to know

Is Lantern Keeper really free?

Yes. The whole game is free, with no ads. A few cosmetic items can be bought, and they never change how you play.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play everything without one, and your progress is saved on your device. A free account adds cloud saves across devices, friends, online co-op, ranked PvP and the Bazaar.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The apps play offline, and the web version does too once you add it to your home screen or install it from your browser. Your progress syncs when you're back online.

How do I play with friends?

Open Co-op. Online, one keeper hosts a room and shares its code. On the same Wi-Fi you can play without an account, or pair two phones with a QR code. Up to four keepers can share a lighthouse.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

The easiest way is the web version in Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it plays full screen and offline. The app itself is available for sideloading on the Downloads page. It is not on the App Store yet.
