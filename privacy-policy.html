Lantern Keeper is made and sold by Exenova ("we", "us"), an independent developer in the Philippines, who decides how your information is used (the personal information controller). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, where it is kept, for how long, and the choices you have. Contact: l4nternkeeper@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play the whole game without an account. Then your progress stays on your device and we receive nothing.
  • If you create an account, we keep your email address, your keeper name and a copy of your save so you can play on more than one device.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no tracking tools, and we never sell your data. Anonymous gameplay statistics are sent only if you agree, never with your name, email or account, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • If you use the social features, we keep your friends list and parties. Community and region chat is a daily conversation, deleted at the end of each day (UTC); party chat and whispers are kept for 30 days. You can block, mute and report other players, and choose who may challenge you to a duel.
  • If you post in the community on our website, your posts, replies, pictures, reactions and poll votes are public, under your keeper name if your profile is shown (otherwise as "A keeper"). You can delete them; they are gone for good 30 days later.
  • If you trade with a friend or post on the Market, we keep a record of the trade or listing (who, what and when) for one year, to keep everyone's items honest. Other signed-in players see your keeper name on your open Market listings.
  • You can switch cloud saves off, hide your profile, choose which emails you get (all optional ones are off unless you turn them on, and one click in any email turns them all off), or delete your account and its data from inside the game at any time.

1. Playing without an account (guest play)

Your progress and settings (embers, upgrades, unlocks, records, the items in your satchel, your player name, sound, graphics and control settings, recent replays) are saved on your device only. On the desktop app a backup copy is kept in the app's own folder on your computer. We never receive this data. If you use Save transfer, the game shows your save as a code, which goes only where you paste it.

If you install the web version from your browser, the browser keeps the game's own files on your device so it can start without a connection. That stores the game, not information about you, and sends us nothing.

The Notification Center (the bell on the home screen, from game version 0.30) keeps its list on your device only: the last 60 notices (our announcements, and things that happened in your game, such as achievements, friend requests and invitations), which ones you have read, and which categories you chose in Settings. To show our announcements the game asks our server for the ones running now; that request carries no information about you beyond what any connection does (see "Technical data" below). The game also keeps a small check value beside your save, to notice a save that was changed outside the game; it is part of the save and stays on your device.

3. Purchases

Anything sold in Lantern Keeper is cosmetic or a supporter item: it changes how things look, never how strong you are. Purchases are made through Google Play and are covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy. When you buy, the game sends the purchase token to our server, which asks Google whether it is genuine before giving you the item. Each purchase can be used by one account only. Our server regularly asks Google which purchases were refunded or cancelled; for those, it takes back what the purchase gave (the Lumens of a refunded pack, never below zero, or the items of a refunded pack) and records this in your Lumens history.

Lumens, the premium currency, are kept with your account: your balance and a history of each change (a pack bought, with its receipt reference; a look bought with Lumens, and when). We keep them so your Lumens are the same on every device and so we can answer questions about a purchase; they are deleted with your account, except purchase records the law requires us to keep.

4. Co-op play

Co-op is optional. When you play co-op, your player name, the keeper you play as and your game actions (taps, abilities, and if you host, what happens in the night) are sent to the other players in your game. They travel either:

  • phone to phone, directly between the devices on your local network, with no server in between (the pairing code contains your device's local network address so the other device can reach it); or
  • through a co-op server ("relay") at the address you or your host enter. The relay passes messages between the players in a room, keeps rooms in memory only while a game is on, and uses connection addresses in memory to limit abuse. It does not store your messages or your name. A relay run by someone else is covered by that person's own policy.
  • through our online service (the Online tab, for signed-in players): the game uses Supabase Realtime to pass game messages between the players who share a room code. Messages are relayed live and are not stored; the room exists only while the game is on.

In a ranked PvP match, the game sends your keeper name, loadout (keeper, weapon, charms, lighthouse), the upgrades you pick and your duel actions (taps and ability use) to your opponent over our online service, while the match is on. These live messages are not stored. Practice matches against an AI keeper stay on your device.

A co-op server can also run the night itself ("server-run co-op"): then every player's game sends its actions to the server, and the server works out the night and sends what happens to everyone. It uses the same information as above, holds it in memory only while the night is on, and stores none of it.

Our co-op server (the Online tab, from game version 0.28): online rooms are normally run by our own co-op server, hosted by Render. It works as described above: it receives your keeper name, the keeper you play as and your game actions, uses your connection's IP address in memory to limit abuse, and stores none of it; the room and the night exist only while the game is on. Its address comes with the game or from a small file on our website (service.json), which the game reads when you open Co-op.

The Lantern Bazaar (the walkable meeting place): while you are in it and signed in, the game shares your keeper name, your keeper's look and where you walk with the other signed-in players in the Bazaar, live over our online service. It is not stored. Players you have blocked don't see you, and you don't see them.

In the co-op lobby (from game version 0.29), the players in your room also see whether you are ready, the quality of your connection to the room (worked out from how long messages take), and which kind of controls and device you play with (for example "controller" or "touch", and "Android" or "desktop"), so everyone can tell who is set. This travels with the other co-op messages and is not stored.

Local and phone-to-phone co-op do not use your account. No kind of co-op ever shows your email address to anyone. Signed-in players you play co-op with are added to each other's recent players list (see above).

5. Anonymous gameplay statistics (only if you agree)

From game version 0.30 the game can send us anonymous statistics so we can see what to improve: for example where nights get too hard, which screens players leave straight away, or errors on a kind of device. It asks once, after your third night, and the choice is in Settings > Privacy. Nothing is sent unless you say yes, with or without an account.

  • What is sent: how each night ended (mode, wave or region, how long it took, the cause, the upgrades picked); a screen you left within three seconds; when you opened a Bazaar gate; an error message, without the technical trace that can contain your computer's file names; one line per launch; and, only if you write it, your feedback (a rating and up to 500 characters). With each batch: the game version and the kind of device (Android, desktop or web; the system; a rounded screen size; the graphics setting; the language).
  • What is never sent: your name, email, account, friends, chat or location. Events carry a random number made for this install of the game, so we can count installs; it is not linked to your account. Our server removes email addresses, links and long numbers from feedback before keeping it, and refuses fields that look like a name or contact detail.
  • How long: 90 days, then deleted automatically. Turning statistics off deletes everything this install has sent. We only ever look at totals (for example "30% of nights in this region are won"), never one player.
  • Younger players: the question asks players under 18 to check with a parent first.

The statistics are stored with Supabase (see below) and used only to improve the game: never for advertising, never sold, and never combined with your account.

6. Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use these service providers, who process it for us and only on our instructions:

  • Supabase hosts the accounts database, sign-in and the save service. Data may be stored or processed in a country other than yours; it is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Google Play handles payments in the Google Play version of the app and tells our server whether a purchase is genuine.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications to the Android app, if you turned them on: it receives your phone's push token and the notice (for example "Keeper wants to be friends"), under Google's Firebase privacy terms.
  • PayPal handles payments in our web store (from game version 0.30): it receives what you enter on its checkout page, and tells our server the order's amount and whether it was paid or refunded, under its own privacy statement.
  • hCaptcha runs the human check on the sign-up, sign-in and password forms (in the game and on our website), to stop automated accounts and password guessing. While you complete the check it receives your IP address and information about your browser or device, under its own privacy policy. It is not used anywhere else in the game.
  • Render hosts our co-op server (see section 4). Like any host it handles your connection's IP address while you are connected, and may keep its own short-lived connection logs under its own privacy policy; it receives nothing else about you from us.
  • GitHub hosts this website, the web version of the game and the downloads. When you visit or download, GitHub receives your IP address and browser information under its own privacy statement.
  • Email delivery: Gmail (Google) sends the codes that confirm your email address and reset your password; Brevo sends the optional emails you chose (patch notes and updates, events, messages from friends you missed, and promotions for adults). Each receives your email address, your keeper name and the message, under its own privacy policy. We keep a record of each optional email sent or failed (the kind, when, and to which account; not the message) for one year, and a list of addresses that must not be written to again (for example after a bounce).
  • The website's address (lk.exenova.is-local.host) is a free name from is-local.host that points to GitHub's servers. It only answers the address lookup your browser makes; your visit itself goes to GitHub.

On our website (from game version 0.30) you can sign in with the same account. Your browser keeps the sign-in in its own storage on your device (not a cookie) until you sign out. Your public page there shows, if your profile is shown, your keeper name, "about you" text, Discord name, roles (Supporter, Club, Veteran, Contributor, Moderator, Developer, Owner), when you joined, your friend code, and how many posts, followers and followed keepers you have. If your profile is hidden, your posts show "A keeper" and no supporter roles. Anyone can read the community, even without an account.

If you choose to show your profile, other signed-in players can see your keeper name, friend code, title, avatar, frame, banner, badges, the achievements and challenges you choose to show, and a summary of your progress (such as level, regions cleared, time played, highest Ascension and how many keepers you have played co-op with). Never your email address.

While you are signed in, the game records when you were last active (checked every few minutes). If your profile is shown, friends and other players see only a rough status next to your name: online (active in the last 10 minutes), today or away. Never the exact time. Hiding your profile hides this status too.

From game version 0.29 the game also records what you are doing in broad terms (at home, in a night, in co-op, in PvP or in the Bazaar), updated at most twice a minute while you are signed in. Only your friends see it, only while you are online, and only if your profile is shown. It replaces the previous value; no history is kept.

If your profile is shown, the ranked leaderboard lists your keeper name, rating and wins and losses once you have played a ranked match. Hiding your profile takes you off the leaderboard.

Chat is seen by the people in that channel: your party, everyone in community or region chat (that day's conversation only), or the one player you whisper. Messages pass an automatic filter for slurs and spam before they are sent. Moderators (people we choose to help keep chat safe) can read reported messages and recent chat to handle reports, and can remove messages and mute players.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud or abuse.

7. Your choices and controls

In the game, under Profile > Account and sync, you can:

  • turn cloud saves off: your progress then stays on this device only;
  • hide your profile from other signed-in players;
  • in Social, block a player (they can no longer message, befriend or invite you, and you don't see their messages), mute a player on your device, report a message or player, remove friends and recent players, leave a party, and choose whether your new achievements are announced in chat (off unless you turn it on), and who may challenge you to a duel (everyone, friends or nobody);
  • under Emails, choose each kind of optional email: patch notes and updates, events, messages from friends you missed (at most one a day), and promotions (only for players who said they are 18 or over). All are off unless you turn them on; otherwise the game only emails you account codes. Every email we send also has a one-click link that turns all optional emails off without signing in;
  • change your email address or password, and sign out;
  • in Settings > Notifications, choose which kinds of notice appear in the Notification Center and pop up (important notices, such as maintenance, always show), and in Settings > Privacy, turn anonymous statistics on or off and send us feedback;
  • delete your account. This permanently deletes your account, cloud save, profile and items straight away. Your progress on the device you are using stays there as a guest save unless you clear it. Purchase records are kept without your account, as described above.

You can also email l4nternkeeper@gmail.com to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it if you can no longer sign in. We may ask you to confirm you own the account. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or Californian law) you may have further rights, including to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases, where the law asks for them: providing the game and account you asked for (contract); keeping the service secure and fair (legitimate interests); optional emails and anonymous statistics (consent, which you can withdraw at any time); purchase records (legal obligation).

8. Security

Connections to our servers use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Each player can read only their own account data, and saves can be changed only through our save service, which checks them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your information we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Lantern Keeper is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for players aged 13 or over. The sign-up form asks for the birth month and year, refuses anyone under 13, and for players aged 13 to 15 asks that a parent or guardian agrees first (our server checks the same answers). We keep only those answers, never the birth date. Younger players can play as a guest, which sends us nothing (unless they agree to anonymous statistics, which the game asks them to check with a parent first). Promotions are offered only to players who said they are 18 or over. In the community, players aged 13 to 17 should never share personal details; moderators remove posts that do. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

10. Android permissions

  • Internet: for signing in, cloud saves, purchases and co-op.
  • Camera (optional): only to scan a co-op pairing QR code. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent. You can paste codes instead.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Notifications (optional, from game version 0.30): asked only when you turn on push notifications in Settings > Notifications. Android asks you to allow them; you can turn them off there or in Android's settings.
  • Game controllers: read only while the game is open, to play with them. Nothing about them is sent to us.
