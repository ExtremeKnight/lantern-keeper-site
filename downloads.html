The fastest way to play is in your browser: nothing to install, and you can add it to your home screen or desktop from the browser's menu to play offline. Your progress follows you between all of these when you sign in.

Android

Allow your browser or file manager to install apps when Android asks (Settings, Install unknown apps). Updates install over the top and keep your progress.

  • Android 7 and newer (.apk) 6 MB

iPhone and iPad

The easiest way on iPhone and iPad is the web version: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It plays offline and full screen. The app file below is for sideloading: AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly sign it with your own Apple ID (free accounts need to refresh it every 7 days). It is not on the App Store yet.

  • For sideloading (.ipa, unsigned) 1 MB

Windows

Windows may say it "protected your PC" because these builds aren't code-signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway.

  • Windows 10 and 11 (most PCs) 79 MB
  • Windows on ARM (Snapdragon laptops) 85 MB

macOS

These builds aren't notarized by Apple yet. After the first try to open it, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

  • Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer) 95 MB
  • Macs with an Intel processor 99 MB

Linux

For the AppImage: make it executable (chmod +x), then run it.

  • AppImage (any distribution) 104 MB
  • Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (.deb) 72 MB
  • Fedora, openSUSE (.rpm) 72 MB
