1. Who can play

Anyone can play as a guest. To create an account you must be at least 13. 2. Your account

  • Give a real email address you can read: it is how you confirm the account and recover it.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else is using it.

  • One person, one account. Don't sell, trade or share accounts.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Account and sync.

3. Fair play

Don't cheat in ways that affect other players or our service. In particular, don't:

  • send edited or impossible saves to our servers, or try to get around the checks that correct them;
  • attack, overload, probe or reverse engineer our servers, or read data that isn't yours;
  • use bugs to get paid items for free, or ask for refunds for items you keep using;
  • harass other players, including in co-op and chat;
  • in ranked PvP: report a result that didn't happen, play on someone else's account, arrange results with another player, or use tools that tap or play for you. Leaving a ranked match counts as a loss. We may reset ratings or remove players from the leaderboard for this.

What you do in your own offline game is up to you (the game even has a debug mode), but saves sent to our servers are checked and may be corrected. If you break these rules we may correct your save, suspend or close your account. Where we close an account for a serious breach, we don't refund items bought for it, unless the law says we must.

4. Game currencies and items

  • Embers, shards, upgrades and other things you earn in the game have no money value. They can't be sold, exchanged for money, or moved to another account, and we may rebalance them as the game changes.
  • Items you buy are cosmetic or supporter items. They never give an advantage in the game. What you buy is a licence to use the item in Lantern Keeper on your account, not ownership of it.
  • Lumens are a premium currency you buy with money and spend on looks. They only ever buy cosmetic items, never an advantage. Like other game currencies they have no money value outside the game: they can't be sold, exchanged for money or moved to another account, and they are kept in your account on our servers. A purchase with Lumens is final once confirmed; if a payment is refunded, cancelled or reversed (by Google Play, by PayPal, or by us), we take back what it gave (the Lumens of a pack, or the items of a supporter pack).
  • In the version of the app from Google Play, purchases are made through Google Play and follow Google Play's payment and refund terms. Everywhere else (Windows, macOS, Linux, the web version and the Android app from our website) they are made in our web store (section 4b). The iPhone and iPad app sells nothing. Purchases are tied to your account and come with it to every device you sign in to.
  • If you are under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission before buying anything.
  • Supporter packs (Supporter, Starter, Dedicated and Founder, in our web store) are one-time purchases of looks, profile items, a title and Lumens. They are a way to support the game and never give an advantage.
  • The Supporter Club is a monthly membership. With PayPal it renews every month at the price shown until you stop it: in the game (Shop > Supporter), on the website (Store) or in your PayPal account. When you stop it, the membership runs to the end of the month already paid and then ends. A month paid by GCash or bank transfer does not renew by itself. While you are a member you have the Club frame, badge and title and the Club role in the community; each month you are a member you also receive that month's lantern, which stays yours after the membership ends. Club perks are recognition and looks only; we may change what a future month brings, and we tell you in the game before a change to the price, which applies only from your next renewal.
  • Boosts, keepsakes, materials and event items are earned in the game (boosts can also be bought with embers or made from materials at the Workbench). Boosts help only in PvE nights, never in PvP. None of them is ever sold for money or Lumens.
  • Trading and the Market: you can trade items with your friends through your Trade chest, or swap them with any player on the Market. A trade happens only when both players have confirmed the same offers, and then it is final. A Market listing holds the items it gives until someone takes it, you cancel it or it ends after 7 days (then the items come back to your chest); taking a listing is final. Traded items have no money value: don't buy, sell or trade them (or accounts) for real money or anything outside the game. We may reverse a trade, or remove items, that came from a bug, cheating or fraud. Items in your Trade chest are part of your account and are deleted with it.
  • If we ever stop offering an item or the online service, we will give reasonable notice in the game. Anything required by the consumer law of your country stays in place: these terms don't take away rights you have by law.

4a. Refunds

Bought through Google Play: refunds are handled by Google Play under its own refund policy (see Google Play's refund help); ask Google Play.

Bought in our web store: write to l4nternkeeper@gmail.com within 14 days of buying, with your order's date and amount. We refund a Lumen pack if its Lumens haven't been spent, and the Supporter Pack in full, to the way you paid (PayPal, your card through PayPal, GCash or your bank). A payment made by mistake (charged twice, or the wrong amount) is always refunded. After 14 days, or for Lumens already spent, we refund only where the consumer law of your country gives you a right to it.

If a purchase is refunded, cancelled or reversed, we remove what it gave (the Lumens from a pack, never taking your balance below zero, or the Supporter Pack's items). Lumens already spent on a look are not refunded by us, because the purchase with Lumens is final once you confirm it. Before any purchase or spending of Lumens, the game shows the price and asks you to confirm.

5. Co-op, chat and the community

Co-op connects you with people you choose: directly, through our online service, or through a relay server. If you use a relay run by someone else, we don't control it. Don't share personal information in your player name or in chat.

In chat, whispers, parties and your profile, be kind. Don't:

  • harass, threaten, bully or stalk anyone, or post hate speech, sexual content or anything illegal;
  • spam, advertise, or post links to scams or cheats;
  • pretend to be another player, a moderator or us;
  • ask other players for passwords, codes, personal details or payment.

Use Block and Report in Social when someone breaks these rules. Messages are filtered automatically and reviewed by moderators when reported. We may remove messages, mute you in chat for a time, or suspend or close your account, depending on how serious the breach is. Chat is offered as it is, and what other players write is their responsibility, not ours.

The community on our website (from game version 0.30) follows the same rules, and also: post in the right section, don't post other people's work as your own, and keep spoilers and personal details out of titles. Posts, replies, pictures and polls are public: anyone can read them, even without an account. You keep the rights to what you post, and you give us permission to show it on the website and to quote it, with your keeper name, in the game's news and our announcements. Report anything that breaks the rules: moderators can hide, lock or remove posts and replies, and we may stop an account from posting. The Supporter Lounge is for Supporter Club members. Roles (Supporter, Club, Veteran, Contributor, Moderator, Developer, Owner) are shown next to names; Contributor and Moderator are given by us and can be taken away. Ideas you share in Feedback or Feature requests may be used in the game without payment or credit unless we agree otherwise with you.
