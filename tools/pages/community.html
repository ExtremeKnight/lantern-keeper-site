Community

Keepers, together

Lantern Keeper is complete on your own, and better with friends. Everything here is inside the game, free, and needs a free account only where it says so.

Friends and parties

Add friends by their friend code, see who is online and what they are up to, and gather in a party with its own chat. (Account)

Co-op for up to four

Defend one lighthouse together. Join online with a room code, play phone to phone nearby, or on your own network. The lobby shows who is ready and how good each connection is.

Duels and ranked PvP

Challenge a friend to a friendly duel that never touches your rating, or climb the ranked leaderboard against keepers matched to your level. (Account)

The Lantern Bazaar

A small 3D harbour where keepers meet between nights: show off your look, trade with friends, post on the Market and craft at the Workbench. (Account)

Daily chat

Community and region chat is one conversation a day, fresh every midnight (UTC). Party chat and whispers stay with your friends.

Community events

Festivals and community goals: every keeper's nights count towards one shared total, shown in the game while the event runs.

Keeping it friendly

  • Block anyone: they can no longer message, befriend, invite or challenge you, and you stop seeing them, in chat and in the Bazaar.
  • Mute a player on your device, or turn the other keeper's reactions off during PvP.
  • Report a message or a player from their name. Moderators see the reported message even after the daily chat has gone.
  • Choose who can challenge you to a duel: everyone, friends only (the default) or nobody. You can also hide your profile.
  • Chat refuses slurs and links before anything is sent, hides rude words, and slows down spam. Please never share personal details.

The full rules are in the Terms of Service, and what is kept and for how long is in the Privacy Policy.
