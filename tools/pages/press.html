About the game

Every night the fog rolls in and its creatures come for the light. Your lantern fires by itself; you strike, choose an upgrade after each wave and use your keeper's ability when it matters. Between nights you build up the Workshop, unlock keepers, weapons and charms, and travel a coast of regions, each with its own creatures and a boss. Play alone, with friends in co-op, or against another keeper in PvP, and meet other keepers in the Lantern Bazaar, a small 3D harbour.

Brand basics

  • Write the name as Lantern Keeper: two words, both capitalised.
  • Use the logo files as they are: no recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects. Leave clear space around the wordmark.
  • Colours: lantern gold #ffcf66, deep gold #ffb938, night #0a0f24, fog blue #121a3a, ember #ff8a3d, sky #7cc8ff.
  • Titles use the Jersey 10 typeface (SIL Open Font Licence).
  • Screenshots may be used in articles, videos and reviews about the game. Please don't edit them to show things the game doesn't have.
