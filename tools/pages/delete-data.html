Delete your data

You can delete your Lantern Keeper account and its data at any time, yourself, in a minute. This page explains how, and what happens to it.

How to delete your account

On the website:

  1. Open Your account and sign in the way you made the account (email and password, or Google, Discord, Twitch, Facebook or X).
  2. Choose Delete my account at the bottom of your account card.
  3. Confirm: type your password, or, for an account made with Google, Discord, Twitch, Facebook or X, press Confirm with and sign in with it again. Tick the box and press Delete my account.

In the game: open Profile > Account, choose Delete account and confirm the same way.

By email: if you can't sign in any more, write to l4nternkeeper@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with "Delete my account" as the subject. We confirm that the address is yours, delete the account and reply when it is done, within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

Deleting removes, at once: your account and sign-in, your profile and keeper name, your cloud save, your friends and parties, your community posts, replies and messages, your Lumens and the looks and items linked to the account, your email choices and your place on the leaderboards. Items you bought can't be restored after deletion.

What stays, without any link to your account: records of purchases (the amount, date and order number), because tax law and refunds need them; results of PvP matches and reports already sent to our safety team; and chat messages, which keep the name they were sent under until chat clears them as usual (community and region chat at the end of each day, party chat and whispers after 30 days). Progress saved on your own devices stays there: it is yours, and you can keep playing as a guest.

Accounts made with Google, Discord, Twitch, Facebook or X

When you sign in with one of them, we use your email address and an account number from it, to recognise you. It also sends the name and picture of that account: they are stored with your sign-in, never shown or used, and deleted with it. We get nothing else (no friends, contacts or posts) and never post anything for you.

Removing Lantern Keeper from that service's settings (for example Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites) stops it signing you in, but doesn't delete your Lantern Keeper account or its data: use the steps above for that.
